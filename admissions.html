💳 Fee Structure & Payment FAQs

Our fee structure is transparent with no hidden costs. Fee components include tuition, learning kit materials, annual event access, and activity charges. Instalment plans are available upon request during campus visits.

⏰ What are the visiting hours for parents?

Parents can visit our campus for guided tours Monday through Friday between 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM (morning) and 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM (evening).
