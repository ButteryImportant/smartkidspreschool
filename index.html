Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the school timings?

Our morning batch operates from 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM, and our afternoon session operates from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

What is the teacher-to-student ratio?

We maintain a strict 1:10 ratio with dedicated support attendants to ensure every child gets personal care and attention.

Are CCTV cameras installed on campus?

Yes, our entire campus including classrooms, play areas, and corridors is under 24/7 CCTV surveillance for safety.
