Playgroup Program

Age Group: 1.5 – 2.5 Years

Our Playgroup curriculum provides a smooth transition from home to school. We focus on sensory exploration, social bonding, building confidence, and basic emotional security.

  • Sensory-rich activities (clay, water play, finger painting)
  • Basic vocabulary building & rhyme recitation
  • Gross motor skill development through outdoor play
  • Encouraging independent eating and self-care habits
Apply for Playgroup
Batch Timings Morning: 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM
Teacher to Student Ratio 1 Teacher : 8 Students (+ Attendant)
Key Focus Areas Socialization, Sensory Play, Speech Readiness

Nursery Program

Age Group: 2.5 – 3.5 Years

Nursery introduces structured routine learning while keeping curiosity at the forefront. Children explore colors, shapes, early letter recognition, and cooperative group games.

  • Introduction to phonics sounds and pre-writing tracing
  • Numerical recognition (1 to 10) & spatial awareness
  • Creative art, music movement, and storytelling
  • Building concentration and listening skills
Apply for Nursery
Batch Timings Morning: 8:30 AM – 11:30 AM
Teacher to Student Ratio 1 Teacher : 10 Students
Key Focus Areas Phonics Sounds, Pattern Writing, Creative Expression

Junior KG Program

Age Group: 3.5 – 4.5 Years

Junior KG bridges foundational play with academic readiness. Children begin two-letter blending, structured handwriting, basic addition concepts, and environmental science.

  • CVC word blending & sight word recognition
  • Number concepts, counting (1-50), and basic shapes
  • Environmental awareness (Plants, Animals, Helpers)
  • Group projects & public speaking activities
Apply for Junior KG
Batch Timings Morning: 8:30 AM – 12:00 PM
Teacher to Student Ratio 1 Teacher : 12 Students
Key Focus Areas Reading Fluency, Numerical Writing, Science Concepts

Senior KG Program

Age Group: 4.5 – 5.5 Years

Our Senior KG program ensures your child enters primary school with total confidence, advanced reading capabilities, problem-solving skills, and independent thinking.

  • Sentence reading, comprehension & creative writing
  • Addition, subtraction & logical sequence puzzles
  • General knowledge & global awareness topics
  • Primary school transition prep & mock routines
Apply for Senior KG
Batch Timings Morning: 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM
Teacher to Student Ratio 1 Teacher : 12 Students
Key Focus Areas Primary School Prep, Logical Math, Sentence Reading

Daycare & After-School Care

Age Group: 2.0 – 8.0 Years

A safe, comfortable, and supervised after-school haven for children of working parents. Features nutritious meal routines, nap areas, homework support, and free play.

  • Hygienic, air-conditioned rest/nap quarters
  • Homework guidance and reading support
  • Supervised indoor play and activity corners
  • Flexible hourly or monthly enrollment options

Our Method

5 Pillars of Our Curriculum

🗣️

Phonics & Literacy

Building strong phonetic sound associations, listening comprehension, and early reading joy.

🔢

Practical Math

Learning numbers through hands-on counting blocks, sorting games, and spatial awareness activities.

🎨

Creative Arts

Fostering imagination through drama, music, rhythm instruments, and fine art exploration.

🧩

Motor Skills

Developing fine motor muscles for pencil grip alongside gross motor balance through obstacle courses.

A Day at Smart Kids

Our Typical Daily Routine

08:30 AM - 09:00 AM

Welcome & Warm-up Assembly

Morning greetings, national anthem, light stretching, and setting the day's positive intention.

09:00 AM - 10:00 AM

Core Literacy & Phonics Circle

Interactive letter sound learning, flashcards, storytelling, and structured writing practice.

10:00 AM - 10:30 AM

Healthy Snack & Manners Break

Promoting hand hygiene, table etiquette, sharing habits, and independent eating.

10:30 AM - 11:30 AM

Sensory & STEM Activity Time

Hands-on math games, science experiments, art corners, and building block challenges.

11:30 AM - 12:30 PM

Outdoor Play & Story Wrap-Up

Physical games, balance beams, reflection on the day's learning, and departure preparation.
