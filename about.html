About Smart Kids Preschool

A home away from home in Kharghar where every child is loved, protected, and encouraged to reach their unique potential.

Our Purpose & Philosophy

Founded with the vision to redefine early childhood education in Kharghar, Smart Kids Preschool provides an environment where learning occurs naturally through discovery and joy.

We believe that early years shape lifelong confidence. That is why our curriculum focuses as much on empathy, emotional resilience, and communication as it does on foundational academics.

Principal's Message

Ms. Manisha Bhume — Principal, Smart Kids Preschool

"Every child comes into the world with unlimited potential and curiosity. Our responsibility as educators is not to fill a vessel, but to ignite a lifelong flame of curiosity and confidence. At Smart Kids, we walk alongside every child and family to make those early years truly count."

Our Principal

Mrs. Manisha Bhume
Principal — Smart Kids Preschool
M.Sc. B.Ed. TTC 10+ Years

Mrs. Manisha Bhume, Principal of Smart Kids Preschool, is a highly qualified and accomplished educationist with an M.Sc., B.Ed. and TTC, and over 10 years of dedicated experience in the field of early childhood education. Known for her exceptional leadership, professionalism and all-round abilities, she has played an integral role in creating a nurturing, engaging and values-driven learning environment for young minds.

A warm, compassionate and inspiring personality, Mrs. Bhume is everyone's favourite and especially loved by the children for her approachable nature and genuine affection towards them. Beyond education, she is also actively involved in social work and community initiatives, reflecting her strong sense of responsibility towards society. Her dedication, grace and commitment have earned her the trust, admiration and affection of students, parents, teachers and the community alike.

Parental Peace of Mind

Our 5-Point Safety Guarantee

📹 24/7 CCTV Monitoring

Complete camera coverage across classrooms, activity zones, and entrance corridors.

🧸 Child-Proofed Spaces

Soft rounded furniture corners, non-slip flooring, and finger-guard door hinges throughout.

🧼 Daily Sanitization

Multiple daily sanitization rounds for toys, learning mats, and washroom facilities.

🩺 First-Aid Certified Staff

Teachers and support staff are trained in pediatric first aid and emergency response.

Explore Our Facilities

Air-Conditioned & Naturally Lit Classrooms

Our classrooms at Sai World Empire (Sector 36) are designed with vibrant colors, ergonomic child-sized furniture, and low-level shelf access to encourage independent exploration.

Features soft foam play equipment, sensory balance beams, and gross motor fun tools where children can play safely regardless of outside weather conditions.
